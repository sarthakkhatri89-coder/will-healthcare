Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Get quick answers to the most common questions about our PCD Franchise program, Third-Party Manufacturing services, and partnership opportunities.

🏭 Third-Party Manufacturing

We manufacture across all major dosage forms: Tablets (Film Coated, Enteric, Chewable, SR, Bilayered), Capsules (Beta-Lactam & Non-Beta, Hard Gelatin, Pellet-filled), Syrups & Suspensions, Dermaceuticals (Creams, Ointments, Lotions, Gels), Cosmetics (Sunscreens, Serums, Shampoos, Soaps), and Dry Syrups / Sachets.

We offer flexible MOQs suited for both start-up brands and large-volume commercial enterprises. Typical MOQs start from 5,000–10,000 units for tablets and capsules, and 1,000–3,000 units for topical and liquid formulations. Contact us for a custom quote tailored to your batch requirements.

Our standard turnaround is 20–30 days from order confirmation and artwork approval. This includes raw material procurement, batch production, multi-stage QC testing, packaging, and dispatch with a complete Certificate of Analysis (COA).

Absolutely. We specialize in private-label / white-label manufacturing. You provide your brand name and artwork — we handle everything from formulation, production, packaging (Alu-Alu, Blister, Bottles, Tubes), to final dispatch under your brand identity with complete confidentiality (NDA signed).

Yes, our Beta-Lactam production operates in a completely independent building with its own dedicated HVAC system, 100% HEPA filtration, segregated pressure cascades, personnel airlocks, and separate material entry/exit — ensuring absolute zero cross-contamination in compliance with Schedule M and GMP requirements.

🤝 PCD Pharma Franchise

Under our District Monopoly PCD model, you receive exclusive distribution rights for your chosen district or territory. This means zero internal competition — no other Will Healthcare franchise will be appointed in your area, giving you full market ownership and maximum profit potential.

Every franchise partner receives a comprehensive Free Promotional Kit including: Visual Aid folders, Product literature brochures, MR promotional bags, Visiting cards, Prescription pads, Reminder cards, Catch covers, and Product samples for doctor detailing.

Starting a PCD Franchise with Will Healthcare requires a modest initial investment primarily towards your first product order stock. There are no franchise fees or royalties. The exact amount varies based on the therapeutic categories and territory selected. Contact our franchise team for a detailed investment breakup.

Our portfolio includes 500+ DCGI-approved formulations spanning general medicine, antibiotics, dermatology, cosmetology, trichology, pediatrics, orthopedics, gastroenterology, cardiac care, and nutraceuticals. New molecules are added regularly based on market demand.

Yes, we maintain ready stock of all popular formulations at our warehouse. Orders are processed within 24-48 hours with insured PAN-India delivery through trusted logistics partners. Urgent orders can be fast-tracked upon request.
