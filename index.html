Capsule Manufacturing

High-capacity automated liquid manufacturing lines for pediatric suspensions, digestive tonics, and cough elixirs.

Pediatric Syrups Cough Suspensions Enzyme Tonics Multivitamin Syrups Dry Syrups (Reconstitutable) Oral Drops
✓ Engineering: SS 316L liquid preparation vessels with magnetic stirrers, multi-stage micron filtration, automatic bottle washing, volumetric filling, ROPP capping & induction sealing.

✓ Key Examples: Wilcof-D Cough Syrup, Prewil Multivitamin Tonic, Pediatric Paracetamol & Antiallergic Suspensions.
